The gap

What a busy physio clinic looks like without a system

Before Octor Physio, clinic staff were managing patient care across three separate tools — none of them designed for physiotherapy.

📋
Appointments on paper
Booking conflicts, double entries, and no visibility into which therapist is free — managing a 6-therapist schedule in a register means something always gets missed.
💬
Clinical notes on WhatsApp
Treatment decisions, exercise assignments, and patient progress shared in group chats. No structured history, no report at discharge, no continuity if a patient changes therapist.
📊
Revenue invisible to the owner
No dashboard, no dropout tracking, no session package visibility. The clinic owner only knows the business is growing when they count invoices at month end.
Octor Physio replaces all three with one system — built around the way physiotherapy actually works.
The platform

Everything in one place, nothing you don't need

A calendar that understands physiotherapy appointments

30-minute consultations and 60-minute sessions look different on the calendar. Up to 4 appointments in one slot are all visible. Tap any empty slot to book — therapist and time pre-fill automatically.

  • All therapists side by side — 9 AM to 1 PM, 5 PM to 9 PM
  • Arrived, completed, no-show statuses updated in real time
  • WhatsApp reminder sent to patient automatically on booking
  • Receptionist iPad app for fast front-desk operation

Clinical documentation built for how therapists think

Initial assessments capture the body map, VAS pain score, medical history, lifestyle, and a full treatment plan. Every follow-up is documented. When a consultation ends, the session report goes to the patient's WhatsApp automatically.

  • Pain area body map with marker type, severity, and pattern
  • Treatment phases with session schedules and exercise protocols
  • Medical history captured once, visible on every consultation
  • Session reports sent to patients automatically on completion

Billing that closes in under a minute

One screen, one save. Session packages track usage automatically — the receptionist always knows how many sessions remain. Prices are set by the clinic; receptionists can't change them.

  • Treatment sessions, consultations, packages, procedures — all in one invoice
  • Cash, card, UPI, and Razorpay online link in one flow
  • Branch-specific QR codes for instant in-clinic payment
  • Invoice PDF generated and shareable immediately
The owner view

Everything happening across all branches, at a glance

Total appointments, completions, waiting patients, no-shows, alerts and AI flags — all on one screen without asking anyone to compile a report.
